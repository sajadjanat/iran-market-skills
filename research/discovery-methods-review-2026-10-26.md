## Excluded or corrected material

- Market totals, Instagram commerce figures, online-retail shares and startup-failure statistics without a read original publication/table were excluded.
- Fixed willingness-to-pay discount coefficients, interview ratios, sample counts and recency windows were replaced by decision-specific choices.
- Guarantee/trust coefficients were not treated as causal effects or sales uplift. General CPI was not treated as a sufficient category deflator.
- Clicks, qualified requests, paid transactions, refunds and repeat use remain separate observations. Missing payment tracking is not zero sales.
- MVPs remain available; research methods are selected to reduce the relevant uncertainty. Informal/manual alternatives count even without direct software competitors.

No claim that these changes improve actual customer outcomes follows from source access. Behavioral and packaging checks are recorded separately in the [evaluation record](../evals/results/discovery-methods-2026-10-26/review.md).

# Iranian market source guide

Access review: 2026-10-03 using a research browser. These directories identify potential publishers; numerical claims need the exact publication, table, period and definitions.

| Owner / source | Use | Access at review | Limit |

## Choosing evidence

Start from the decision and relevant segment, not an indiscriminate collection of national statistics. Pair category context with evidence about the actual customer job. For prices record rial/toman, tax/shipping inclusion, access date and eligibility. For time comparisons record nominal versus adjusted values and the chosen adjustment source.

For an inaccessible publication, request an official accessible copy or keep its claims unknown. If using a mirror, record publisher and host separately, exact report identity, access and limitations; do not upgrade it to a verified official publication. Prefer a direct publication URL over a publisher homepage.

Where sources disagree, retain both and compare periods, definitions, sample and units. A national growth indicator and five interviews answer different questions; neither can be silently substituted for the other.

## Customer discovery in a local context

Fill a compact context record: job and recent trigger; actual service area; language/register; acquisition channel; device/access needs; total-cost and payment constraints; decision maker versus payer; relevant period. Keep unknowns explicit. Recruit recent customers, people who abandoned the task and users of substitutes through more than the founder's own social circle where feasible. These are sampling choices, not a representative national panel.

Ask about a specific recent episode before presenting a solution:

- «آخرین بار که به این خدمت نیاز داشتید چه اتفاقی افتاد؟ از کجا شروع کردید؟»
- «بین چه گزینه‌هایی انتخاب کردید؟ هزینهٔ نهایی و شرایط را چطور فهمیدید؟»
- «کجا منصرف شدید یا کمک خواستید؟ چه چیزی هنوز نامشخص بود؟»
- «چه کاری انجام دادید؟» is evidence about an episode; «اگر این اپ باشد می‌خرید؟» is hypothetical intent.

Observe task completion or a consented prototype decision separately from stated enthusiasm. Do not collect personal/payment identifiers to establish demand. An interview count cannot turn a convenience sample into market prevalence.

For commerce opportunities compare serviceable cities, order eligibility, full cost, seller/stock conditions, support and refund terms. [Digikala payment FAQ](https://www.digikala.com/faq/question/81/), [shipping FAQ](https://www.digikala.com/faq/question/79/) and [return FAQ](https://www.digikala.com/faq/question/83/) are candidate first-party policies, not rules for all Iranian businesses. Reopen the relevant page and applicable order conditions; a retrieved page with no update date may still contain stale terms.

For Nowruz, school calendars, religious occasions or weather-related demand, record the particular year/date, region, product mechanism and comparable periods. Obtain the relevant dated calendar and operational schedule before giving deadlines; a holiday name alone neither proves a demand uplift nor common preferences. Test comprehension of the offer, total cost and recovery path with the intended segment.

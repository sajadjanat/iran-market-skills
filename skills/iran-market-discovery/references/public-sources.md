## Choosing evidence

Start from the decision and relevant segment, not an indiscriminate collection of national statistics. Pair category context with evidence about the actual customer job. For prices record rial/toman, tax/shipping inclusion, access date and eligibility. For time comparisons record nominal versus adjusted values and the chosen adjustment source.

For an inaccessible publication, request an official accessible copy or keep its claims unknown. If using a mirror, record publisher and host separately, exact report identity, access and limitations; do not upgrade it to a verified official publication. Prefer a direct publication URL over a publisher homepage.

Where sources disagree, retain both and compare periods, definitions, sample and units. A national growth indicator and five interviews answer different questions; neither can be silently substituted for the other.

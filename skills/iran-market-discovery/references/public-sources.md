# Public starting points for Iranian market discovery

Source status checked 2026-10-03. These are starting points, not an exhaustive directory. Reopen the source before relying on a changing figure.

| Source | Useful for | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| [Statistical Center of Iran](https://www.amar.org.ir/) | Official population, household, and economic publications | Aggregate tables rarely answer demand for one product or segment directly |
| [Central Bank of Iran](https://www.cbi.ir/) | Official economic and monetary publications | Macro indicators are not customer interviews or category conversion data |
| [Iran e-commerce development portal](https://ecommerce.gov.ir/fa) | Reports and publications from the Center for Development of Electronic Commerce | Check report year, metric definitions, and the official publication page; portal access may vary |
| First-party company product pages and official app listings | Public feature descriptions, terms, prices, and availability | Company descriptions are not independent evidence of quality or market share |
| Direct customer interviews, support logs, and analytics supplied by the user | Jobs, objections, trust, price response, and product behavior | Sample and collection method must be stated; remove personal data before publication |

## Evidence record

For every market claim, note: `claim`, `evidence_type`, `source_owner`, `source_url`, `published_or_observed_at`, `scope`, `limitation`, and `confidence`. Link to the source instead of copying a full dataset. If a direct source cannot be opened, report the access issue and avoid filling the gap with a secondary claim presented as certain.

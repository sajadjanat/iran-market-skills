# Decision evidence and experiment records

Use one record per decision-relevant claim. Separate facts from conclusions.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| claim / id | One checkable statement and a stable reference ID |
| evidence_type | Public fact, dated observation, user evidence, inference or hypothesis |
| source_owner / source_url | Original publisher and direct link, or anonymized private artifact ID |
| published_or_updated_at | Verified publication date, or unknown |
| observed_or_retrieved_at | Actual access/observation date |
| access_status | Read, partial, blocked or unavailable; what was accessible |
| period / scope / sample | Reporting period, geography, segment, method and sample if known |
| value / unit | Preserve reported units, denominator and currency |
| limitation / confidence | What it cannot establish; high/medium/low with reason |
| next_test | What could confirm or disconfirm the assumption |

An inference cites the records it combines and explains the reasoning. A hypothesis has no implied source verification. Missing fields remain unknown.

## Validation plan

Record assumption, participant criteria, recruiting channel, method, observable signal, cost/time bound, owner-agreed success threshold, failure threshold and the action after either outcome. If the owner has not agreed a threshold, label it proposed. Interviews explore jobs and objections; actual buying behavior needs an appropriate behavioral test.

### Synthetic example

Fictional brief: a repair-booking service for apartment residents in one city. No research has been run.

- Hypothesis H1: residents struggle to know the final service price.
- Evidence: none yet; interview topic, not a market fact.
- Test: recruit residents with a recent repair experience; ask them to reconstruct the last booking and compare a price-explanation prototype.
- Decision: investigate whether price uncertainty recurs and whether the proposed explanation is understood. Any numerical go/no-go threshold remains an owner decision.

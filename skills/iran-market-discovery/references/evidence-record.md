# Market evidence record

Use one row per decision-relevant claim.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Claim | One checkable statement |
| Evidence type | Public fact, dated observation, user evidence, inference, or hypothesis |
| Source | Owner and direct URL, or anonymized first-party research record |
| Date and scope | Publication/observation date, geography, period, and segment |
| Limitation | What this evidence cannot establish |
| Confidence | High, medium, or low, with one-sentence reason |
| Next test | Cheapest action that could confirm or disconfirm the claim |

Keep opinions in a separate column from source facts. A national or sector report can frame a question; it cannot prove that a specific category, discount, or price point has demand.

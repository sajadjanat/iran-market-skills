# Persian articles that give the reader something usable

Use for blog articles, practical ideas and product tutorials. This is an editorial method, not a fixed template or a claim about every Iranian reader.

## Carry the brief through revisions

Read supplied articles, approved passages and corrections before inventing a voice. Keep a compact working profile: audience/channel, approved register, terms, rejected patterns with reasons, intended reader outcome and verified facts. A correction such as «تعطیل نمیشه، نه تعطیل نمی‌شود» establishes this brief's conversational register; it does not make formal Persian wrong. Apply it consistently to the body and headings. Do not store user material outside the authorized project.

When continuing an approved draft, preserve its useful structure and meaning. A request for images is not a request to rewrite it. Publication follows existing user authorization; ask only for missing facts or authorization actually needed.

## Pick one reader outcome

Complete: “After reading, this person can ___ in ___ circumstances.” Choose a central question and make each section advance its answer. A title is a promise the body must fulfill. If the brief asks for three proposals and sample paragraphs, deliver those before drafting full articles.

| Article kind | What earns its space | Reader's concrete outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Practical idea | Introduce one activity, what makes it enjoyable, setup, an example round and usable variations | Try the activity with the people/materials available |
| Game introduction | Verified player count/materials and rules, a worked turn, tricky cases and ending/scoring | Run a first round without another rules search |
| Product tutorial | Actual prerequisites, current UI labels, meaningful states and observed result | Complete the named task and recognize success/recovery |
| Experience/explainer | A specific question, relevant evidence or attributed experience, explanation and implications | Understand a decision or apply a useful insight |

Adapt length and section order to the subject. Do not force a problem/solution sales funnel, numbered tips or a repeated conclusion onto every genre.

## Build a connected article

1. Open with a recognizable situation or the useful answer. Avoid disposable openings such as «در دنیای پرمشغلهٔ امروز» and assumptions that every reader scrolls too much.
2. Introduce the actual subject early. Explain why it fits this situation through concrete features, not unsupported popularity or exaggerated excitement.
3. Give enough verified detail to act. For a game, show a turn from prompt through decision to outcome; for a tutorial, walk through the real interface. Explain unfamiliar terms where they first matter.
4. Put likely confusion next to the step that causes it. Variations should change a real constraint (number of people, space, time or materials), not repeat generic advice.
5. End with the completed outcome or a small usable next step. A summary that simply repeats headings adds little. A brand CTA is optional and must fit the reader's task.

Use paragraphs to develop thoughts and transitions to connect them. Use lists for rules, materials or real sequences; do not turn the entire article into disconnected bullets. Headings should answer the reader's next question. Remove “do this/don't do that” padding when the reader asked for an actual idea.

## Facts before fluency

Inspect available project documentation, source, approved content and authorized connected data before asking for new access. For tutorials, code or screenshots can support labels but do not prove a successful live flow. Keep those limits visible in the delivery notes. Never invent a button, payment outcome or service promise to complete a narrative.

For real games, use the publisher's rules or a reliable original manual and identify the edition when it matters. Label house rules and hypothetical rounds. Confirm local availability separately; an Iranian listing or translation does not prove widespread use. If critical rules are unavailable, produce a bounded draft with named gaps or choose a verified subject within scope.

Research local examples when the brief needs them: Iranian operators/publishers, primary instructions, dates and actual conditions. A Persian webpage alone is not evidence of cultural fit. Adapt the activity to stated circumstances; do not decorate an imported idea with tea, Yalda or family stereotypes.

## Titles and tone

Draft a few distinct titles only when useful or requested. Each should identify the subject or concrete experience and match what the article actually gives. An evocative title may need a clarifying subtitle. Avoid formulaic “ultimate guide,” guaranteed fun, invented novelty and claims such as “everyone in Iran loves this.” Read title and opening together: do they invite the same reader into the same article?

Conversational prose still needs clear referents, controlled sentence length and consistent terms. Prefer the user's natural forms over ornamental slang or forced intimacy. Avoid mechanically changing every «را» to «رو»: quotes, UI labels, source facts and formal briefs keep their own register.

Original illustrative rewrite (fictional activity; not customer evidence):

- Vague: «با یک فعالیت خلاقانه، لحظاتی فراموش‌نشدنی برای دوستان خود رقم بزنید.»
- Concrete, for a conversational brief: «یه کاغذ بذار وسط و از هر نفر بخواه یه خط به نقاشی اضافه کنه. نفر بعد باید همون شکل رو ادامه بده؛ بعد از یه دور، ببینید هر کدومتون فکر می‌کردید دارید چی می‌کشید.»

The second passage introduces a playable activity. It still needs the rest of the article's setup and example; a friendly sentence alone is not a finished article.

## Images and final read

Choose images by purpose: show setup, clarify a move, locate a real control or mark a meaningful transition. Place them near the corresponding passage. A generated illustration is not an app screenshot or evidence that an event occurred. Distinguish covers from instructional evidence, provide useful alt text and respect reuse rights.

Read once as a new reader: can they name the subject, follow the example, understand unfamiliar terms and reach the promised result? Read once for voice and flow: remove repeated introductions, empty enthusiasm, detached tips and redundant closing paragraphs. Deliver the full requested text, with source/verification notes outside the public-facing prose where appropriate. Do not claim human authorship or guarantee an AI-detector result.

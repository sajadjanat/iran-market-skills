## Brief and working conventions

Record audience/context, placement, action, offer facts, required disclosures, approved vocabulary and liked/rejected brand examples. Choose formal or conversational register from the brief. Preserve brand spellings and the user's dialect/register rather than imposing one voice on all Persian audiences.

For newly drafted standard Persian, use Persian ی/ک, natural punctuation and appropriate word spacing/half-space consistently. Keep identifiers/URLs verbatim. For requested colloquial copy, preserve intentional colloquial forms; spelling guidance and audience preference are different questions.

| Placement | Useful decision |
|---|---|
| Landing heading | Name the concrete benefit without an unsupported superlative |
| CTA | Describe the real next action: viewing options is different from purchasing |
| Error | Explain the problem and the next available recovery step |
| Price/offer | State currency, eligibility and known terms clearly |
| Campaign | Keep date, stock, discount basis and delivery claims factual |

## Claim review

For each promise record approved fact/source, exact qualification and placement. Unknown delivery, guarantee or scarcity terms are omitted or raised for owner input. Deadline and stock claims cannot be invented for urgency. Tone alternatives must retain the same facts.

### Synthetic example

Fictional brief: a repair-request form; visitors submit a request, then receive an estimate. No booking or instant price is guaranteed.

- Too strong: «همین حالا تعمیرکار قطعی با کمترین قیمت رزرو کن»
- Formal: «درخواست تعمیر ثبت کنید و برآورد هزینه بگیرید»
- Conversational: «درخواستت رو ثبت کن تا برآورد هزینه بگیری»
- CTA: «ثبت درخواست»

The rewrite changes voice while preserving the actual action. These are original demonstration lines, not tested conversion claims.

# Persian writing and brand-voice references

Sources checked 2026-10-03. Use sources for the decisions they cover; spelling guidance does not prescribe a brand's personality.

| Source | Use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Academy of Persian Language and Literature: Persian orthography manual (PDF)](https://apll.ir/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Dastour-e-Khat-17.04.1402.pdf) | Standard written Persian spelling and orthographic decisions | Does not determine a product's conversational register or marketing voice |
| [Academy page: proposed guide to informal Persian spelling](https://apll.ir/1403/02/26/%D9%86%D8%B3%D8%AE%DB%80-%D9%BE%DB%8C%D8%B4%D9%86%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%DB%8C-%D8%AF%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%AE%D8%B7-%D9%88-%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%87%D9%86%DA%AF-%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%DB%8C%DB%8C-%D9%81/) | A reference when a brief explicitly requests informal spelling | The Academy labels it proposed; don't present it as binding standard |
| [Unicode CLDR: Persian](https://cldr.unicode.org/translation/language-specific/persian) | Persian letter forms, digits, date and number locale conventions | Locale guidance, not a tone-of-voice guide |

## Brief before drafting

Capture the audience and use context, channel, desired action, offer facts, required disclosures, approved terminology, and example copy the owner likes or rejects. If the audience or voice is unknown, label tone options as hypotheses and recommend a small user check. Preserve truthful price, availability, discount, guarantee, delivery, and deadline statements.

# Persian Brand Copy

Write natural Persian fitting the brief and preserving verified offer facts. Apply the [evidence policy](references/evidence-policy.md). Read the [writing reference](references/persian-writing.md) when choosing orthography/register or checking placement-specific copy.

## Workflow

1. **Read the brief.** Identify audience, channel, desired action, offer facts, brand examples, terminology and real length constraints. Ask for missing facts that change a claim; otherwise record assumptions. Keep approved facts separate from creative choices.
2. **Choose the voice.** Follow supplied register and examples. If unknown, offer a few meaningfully different options labeled by intended effect. Treat audience response as a hypothesis, not a national trait. Finish with a voice choice or labeled alternatives.
3. **Draft for the placement.** Use clear idiomatic Persian, stable terminology and suitable spacing/punctuation. Preserve requested formality, colloquial forms and brand spellings. Align CTAs with the actual action. Interface messages explain the situation and user's next action.
4. **Check claims and meaning.** Verify price/unit, discount basis, availability, eligibility, delivery, guarantees and deadlines against the brief/sources. Remove unsupported promises and invented urgency. For unknown critical terms, ask or draft without that claim. Alternative tones preserve the same offer.
5. **Review the result.** Check requested format/length, flow, orthography, ambiguity and accessible labels. Explain material tone/claim trade-offs briefly. Recommend a specific message test where audience uncertainty affects the decision.

## Deliverable and completion

Deliver copy in the requested format, useful alternatives, brief rationale and unresolved terms. Every offer claim must match an approved fact/source and each requested placement must have usable text. Label a factually incomplete draft; do not call it publication-ready.

Spelling sources guide orthography, not ideal brand voice. For a small edit, preserve scope rather than starting a strategy interview. Publishing or messaging is separate from drafting and follows the user's authorization.

# Persian Brand Copy

Use this skill to produce Persian copy that fits a stated audience, offer, and channel. Correct orthography is not the same as a brand voice, and neither establishes what all Iranian customers prefer.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience, product, desired action, channel, offer terms, and any approved brand examples. Ask for missing facts when they affect a claim; otherwise state the working assumption.
2. Choose a register for this audience and placement. If no voice has been established, show a small set of distinct options rather than inventing a universal Iranian tone.
3. Draft clear, natural Persian. Keep product facts, price, discount, availability, guarantees, delivery promises, and deadlines consistent with supplied or sourced evidence.
4. Use consistent spelling and terminology. Preserve the requested level of formality; do not flatten colloquial speech into formal prose or add slang as a stereotype.
5. Provide concise alternatives when useful and label what each tests (clarity, warmth, urgency, or value). Recommend user testing when the voice or claim materially affects trust.

## Completion

Deliver copy in the requested format, plus a brief rationale and any unverified claim that needs owner review. Read [Persian writing references](references/persian-writing.md) for orthography sources and the boundary between spelling guidance and voice decisions.

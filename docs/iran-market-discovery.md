# Iran market discovery

## When to use it

Choose which customer/job to investigate or which assumption to test before investing in an Iran-focused product. Give category, audience/geography and the decision. Sources and interviews help; their absence changes the output into a research plan.

## Request and synthetic example

```text
با $iran-market-discovery برای سرویس درخواست تعمیر در شیراز
تصمیم بگیر کدام فرضیه را اول بررسی کنیم.
هنوز دادهٔ مشتری یا آمار دسته نداریم؛ فرضیه را واقعیت معرفی نکن.
```

Illustrative output, not completed research:

| Item | Example |
|---|---|
| Decision | Which booking obstacle should be investigated first? |
| Hypothesis | Uncertainty about final price may discourage requests |
| Evidence | Not yet collected; no population claim |
| Next step | Interview recent repair customers and test a price-explanation prototype |
| Decision threshold | Proposed with owner; no invented universal threshold |

Use the [evidence record](../skills/iran-market-discovery/references/evidence-record.md) and [source guide](../skills/iran-market-discovery/references/public-sources.md). [The fictional price fixture](../evals/fixtures/market-brief.json) exercises units, report periods and sampling limits.

## It is working if

The result has a bounded decision, dated/attributed material claims, visible gaps and an experiment whose outcome changes the decision. A national report is not presented as proof of product demand.

## Common limits

Blocked sources are marked, not substituted with invented numbers. Exploratory interviews do not establish population prevalence. Rial/toman and report periods remain explicit. Without browsing the result is a plan or artifact analysis.

## Customer discovery and experiment selection

The [methods reference](../skills/iran-market-discovery/references/discovery-methods.md) adds episode-based interviewing, jobs/switching analysis, assumption prioritization, outcome-to-solution mapping, willingness-to-pay evidence and experiment selection. Use only the parts needed for the decision. It distinguishes author guidance from our adaptations and records exact primary-source URLs and what was actually read; it does not claim full-book or video review.

For a repair service, a compliment does not establish demand and an offer click does not establish a paid booking. The output separates known evidence, interpretations and unknowns; prioritizes an assumption with a reason; and proposes an interview, prototype, manual pilot or priced-offer test with decision criteria. An MVP remains an option. Sample size, recency, solution counts and thresholds depend on context rather than a universal formula. Planning does not authorize recruitment, ads, spending or payments.

### خلاصه فارسی قابلیت های جدید

‏اسکیل کمک میکنه درباره تجربه واقعی مشتری سوال بپرسیم، بفهمیم الان چطور مشکلش رو حل میکنه و چه چیزی میتونه باعث تغییر روشش بشه. بعد فرض های مهمی رو که شواهد کافی ندارن اولویت بندی میکنه و برای بررسیشون یک آزمایش قابل اجرا پیشنهاد میده.

‏تعریف کردن، کلیک، ثبت درخواست و پرداخت رو یکسان حساب نمیکنه. خروجی مشخص میکنه چی میدونیم، چی برداشتمونه و چی هنوز نیاز به بررسی داره. تعداد مصاحبه، زمان تجربه مشتری و معیار تصمیم با شرایط پروژه تنظیم میشن. نتیجه تحقیق به تنهایی مجوز تماس با افراد، انتشار یا دریافت پول نیست.

‏روش ها با مراجعه به صفحات اصلی نویسندگان بررسی شدن؛ ادعای مطالعه کامل کتاب ها یا تماشای ویدیوها نداریم. آمار بدون منبع دقیقِ گزارش پژوهشی وارد اسکیل نشده. نمونه های این راهنما آموزشی هستن، نه نتیجه تحقیق روی مشتری واقعی.

## Local customer research

Record the actual service area, recent customer episode, decision maker/payer, language, channel and relevant access/payment constraints. The [source guide](../skills/iran-market-discovery/references/public-sources.md) now includes neutral Persian interview prompts, recruiting limits and seasonal verification. Ask what people actually did before testing what they might buy.

## Cultural fit for content

The [source guide](../skills/iran-market-discovery/references/public-sources.md) also covers situated cultural research: recent activities, audience constraints, local availability and neutral Persian questions. One owner's article feedback establishes that brand's preferences; it cannot establish what all Iranians enjoy. Ask about actual episodes before choosing a culturally framed idea.
